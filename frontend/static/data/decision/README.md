# Decision evidence sources

Climate and reanalysis: Open-Meteo (CC BY 4.0), https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/climate-api and https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api. Models, periods, locations, units and transformations are in `terra/research/build_decision_data.py`; raw input hashes are in evidence.json. Climate model weather is not historical observation. Reanalysis soil comparison is not independent observational validation.

Synthetic shipment-mode totals and prices: Apetit Climate & Nature Navigator synthetic dataset, Since AI ry 2026 (CC BY 4.0). Licence and limitations are in ../apetit/. Original restricted workbook is not redistributed. No weather-to-yield or supplier-loss calibration is available. Financial outputs are hypothetical arithmetic scenarios, not investment recommendations or actual company figures.
