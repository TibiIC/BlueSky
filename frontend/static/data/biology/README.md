# Measured fungal DNA — Kungsängen pilot

Source: Rosling A, Eshghi Sahraei S, Furneaux B, Kluting K, Zakieh M (2026). Kungsängen soil microbial communities. Version 1.9. Biology Section, Uppsala University. https://doi.org/10.15468/upxbwy

CC0 1.0: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Derived from the pinned public Darwin Core archive. JSON includes archive URL/hash and transformation metadata. Contains 20 historical sample events (2016-06-07) at one shared published coordinate, 1,152 fungal DNA occurrence records. No bacterial coverage, farm association or Finland measurements. No invented coordinate jitter. ASV richness is observed sequence-variant richness, not species richness. Shannon is computed from read proportions, not organism abundances; no rarefaction. Water content is measured gravimetrically (g/g dry soil). These snapshots do not change with animation frames and do not calibrate the regional physics model.

## Finland: Karstula

Source: Adamczyk et al. (2025), https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.13837158, CC BY 4.0. Derived from Karstula_SOM.csv and Karstula_Enzyme_activity.csv. Only 24 Soil rows are included (Root rows excluded), with 192 enzyme measurements. The source study coordinate applies to all samples; no plot locations are invented. Sampling date September 2021 is known to month precision. Reported fungal biomass/necromass are biomarker-based estimates, not DNA richness or a direct comparison with Sweden. JSON preserves source units, URLs and SHA-256 hashes.
